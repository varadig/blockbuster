# Moonshard – itch.io oldal

Az itch.io oldal szövegei, beállításai és képei. A build feltöltése: `scripts/package-itch.ps1` (zip: `dist/moonshard-html5.zip`)
vagy `scripts/publish-itch.ps1` (butler, `itch.config.json`: `"game": "moonshard"`).

## Átállás a Blockbuster névről (egyszeri teendők az itch.io-n)

1. A projekt **Edit game** oldalán: *Title* = `Moonshard`, *Project URL* = `moonshard` (az itch.io átirányítja a régi címet; ha új projektet
   hozol létre, annak URL-je legyen `moonshard`, hogy a `itch.config.json` stimmeljen).
2. Töltsd fel az új zipet (`dist/moonshard-html5.zip`) – vagy futtasd a *Publish to itch.io* feladatot. A régi fájlt töröld, vagy a butler a
   `html5` csatornán felülírja.
3. A feltöltött fájlnál pipáld be: *This file will be played in the browser*.
4. A képek a `docs/itch/` mappában vannak (lásd lent).

## Hosszú leírás

> **Az itch.io leírás-mezője nem Markdown, hanem rich text szerkesztő**: a bemásolt Markdown jelölők (#, **, |) sima szövegként látszanak.
> Használd a **docs/itch/description.html** fájlt: nyisd meg böngészőben, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, majd az itch.io szerkesztőben (Edit game > Description) Ctrl+V.
> A címsorok, listák és a félkövér így formázva kerülnek be. Az alábbi Markdown csak a szöveg forrása / áttekintéshez van itt.

### Forrás (Markdown, csak áttekintéshez)

```markdown
# MOONSHARD

**Dodge. Blast. Survive.**

A moonlit night, a hero, and a sky that will not stop falling. Moonshard is a fast arcade platformer where meteors rain from above:
dodge the falling ones, shoot them apart for points and coins, then use the landed rocks as cover – and watch them roll!

## Features

- **Meteor rain** in four sizes. Some glow red-hot after landing, some are gold and drop power-ups. Landed meteors roll when pushed
  or when they land on each other.
- **10 levels** with their own themes, quests and a **boss at the end of every level** – a tank, a ghost wyrm, a muscle brute, a
  three-eyed watcher, a hairy killer, a giant spider, a fluff golem, a hollow moon… and the **final boss: a giant slime that turns into every
  boss you beat**, and goes berserk when it is almost dead.
- **Weapons and power-ups**: pistol, rockets, lasers, grenades, spread shot, rapid fire and homing shots that stack with each other.
  Hold the charge key for a big shot.
- **Modifiers** that mess with you for a while: trippy mushrooms, pixelation, flashlight-only view, invisibility, tunnel vision,
  tiny hero, magnetic meteors, and an **ASCII mode** that turns the world into colored letters.
- **Three heroes** (balanced, fast and slippery, slow and high-jumping), upgrades and weapons to buy in the shop with the coins you collect.
- **Living night sky**: 1–3 moons with changing phases, shooting stars that announce a meteor fall, a tiny spaceship circling the moon.
- Everything – graphics, sound effects and the tracker-style music – is **generated by code**, no image or sound files.
- Keyboard (**fully rebindable**), gamepad and mouse-driven menus. There is a built-in **Help** screen.

## Controls (default)

| | |
|---|---|
| Move | A D / Arrow keys |
| Jump | Space / Z (hold for a higher jump) |
| Shoot | J / X (hold for rapid fire) |
| Charged shot | hold L / C, release to fire |
| Aim up / diagonal | W / ↑ (+ left or right) |
| Crouch / aim down in the air | S / ↓ |
| Pause | P / Esc |
| Mute | N |
| Fullscreen | F |

Change every key in **Settings → Controls**. Gamepad: stick = move, X = jump, Square = shoot, Circle = charge, Options = pause.

## Tips

- Jump up *through* floating platforms from below; they only hold you from above.
- Crouch to hit low targets and make yourself a smaller target.
- Boss arenas have platforms: jump over the boss from them.
- Weapons you pick up are lost at the end of a level – the one you bought in the shop is your starting weapon.
- Losing all continues ends the run: coins and shop weapons are reset.

Made with Haxe + HaxeFlixel.
```
