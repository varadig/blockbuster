MOONSHARD

Dodge. Blast. Survive.

A moonlit night, a hero, and a sky that will not stop falling. Moonshard is a fast arcade platformer where meteors rain from above: dodge the falling ones, shoot them apart for points and coins, then use the landed rocks as cover – and watch them roll!

Features

  • Meteor rain in four sizes. Some glow red-hot after landing, some are gold and drop power-ups. Landed meteors roll when pushed or when they land on each other.
  • 10 levels with their own themes, quests and a boss at the end of every level – a tank, a ghost wyrm, a muscle brute, a three-eyed watcher, a hairy killer, a giant spider, a fluff golem, a hollow moon… and the final boss: a giant slime that turns into every boss you beat, and goes berserk when it is almost dead.
  • Weapons and power-ups: pistol, rockets, lasers, grenades, spread shot, rapid fire and homing shots that stack with each other. Hold the charge key for a big shot.
  • Modifiers that mess with you for a while: trippy mushrooms, pixelation, flashlight-only view, invisibility, tunnel vision, tiny hero, magnetic meteors, and an ASCII mode that turns the world into colored letters.
  • Three heroes (balanced, fast and slippery, slow and high-jumping), upgrades and weapons to buy in the shop with the coins you collect.
  • Living night sky: 1–3 moons with changing phases, shooting stars that announce a meteor fall, a tiny spaceship circling the moon.
  • Everything – graphics, sound effects and the tracker-style music – is generated by code: no image or sound files.
  • Keyboard (fully rebindable), gamepad and mouse-driven menus. There is a built-in Help screen.

Controls (default)

  • Move: A D / Arrow keys
  • Jump: Space / Z (hold for a higher jump)
  • Shoot: J / X (hold for rapid fire)
  • Charged shot: hold L / C, release to fire
  • Aim up / diagonal: W / Up arrow (plus left or right)
  • Crouch / aim down in the air: S / Down arrow
  • Pause: P / Esc   Mute: N   Fullscreen: F

Change every key in Settings → Controls.
Gamepad: stick = move, X = jump, Square = shoot, Circle = charge, Options = pause.

Tips

  • Jump up through floating platforms from below – they only hold you from above.
  • Crouch to hit low targets and make yourself a smaller target.
  • Boss arenas have platforms: jump over the boss from them.
  • Weapons you pick up are lost at the end of a level – the one you bought in the shop is your starting weapon.
  • Losing all continues ends the run: coins and shop weapons are reset.
